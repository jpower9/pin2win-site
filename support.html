What is a cycle?

In Pin2Win, a cycle is the time between your lab draws. Each lab draw you mark as closing a cycle ends one and starts the next, so you can compare how your labs and how you felt changed after each adjustment your prescriber makes.

Why does my lab say "near trough" or "near peak"?

Pin2Win compares the draw time with your last logged dose of your main therapy: under 25% of your dosing interval is "near peak", 75% or more is "near trough", anything else is "mid-interval". It's a simple rule, not a pharmacokinetic model. Testosterone results drawn at different points of the interval aren't directly comparable — many patients draw at trough, the morning before a scheduled dose. Confirm timing with your prescriber.

Rating from the notification

Touch and hold (or swipe down on) a "Time for your check-in" notification and choose an energy rating. It's saved to today's check-in without opening the app. Tap the notification for the full check-in.

Backups and moving to a new iPhone

Pin2Win keeps everything on your iPhone. Settings → Export backup creates a file you can save to Files or AirDrop to yourself; Settings → Restore from backup brings it back. The file contains your health data — keep it private.

Purchases

Settings → Restore purchases restores Pin2Win Pro on a new device. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Medical disclaimer

Pin2Win is for patients on testosterone therapy prescribed and monitored by a physician. It never calculates or suggests a dose. Patient-reported data for discussion with your physician. Not medical advice.
