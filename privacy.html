Pin2Win Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Pin2Win helps patients on physician-prescribed testosterone therapy track their protocol, doses, check-ins and lab results, cycle by cycle. It is built so that your health information never leaves your iPhone unless you choose to share it. Pin2Win does not collect any data.

What stays on your device

  • What you enter: your protocol and prescribed medications, the doses you log and injection sites, check-ins (energy, libido, mood, sleep and other ratings, side effects, notes), lab results with their draw times and reference ranges, blood donations, costs, supply on hand, body weights, your questions for your doctor and an optional name for your report.
  • Lab-sheet photos (optional): stored only in Pin2Win's own storage on your iPhone, not in your photo library or iCloud.
  • Apple Health (optional): if you allow it, Pin2Win reads body weight, body fat, lean mass, blood pressure, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, steps and exercise minutes to show them next to your check-ins and labs. These readings are kept in memory while the app is open and are not copied into Pin2Win's files. If you turn it on, Pin2Win writes the body weights you enter back to Apple Health.

All of this is stored only in Pin2Win's private storage on your iPhone, using iOS data protection, and can be locked with Face ID. The widget receives only two dates: your next dose day and your planned lab day — no medication names, doses or results. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; their text never names a medication.

What Pin2Win does NOT do

  • No account, sign-in, server or cloud database. Pin2Win does not sync to iCloud.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • Your health data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone — including us. Pin2Win does not generate leads for clinics or anyone else. This is consistent with Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar consumer health-data laws: there is no collection, sharing or sale of consumer health data.

The App Store privacy label for Pin2Win is "Data Not Collected".

Sharing you choose

If you create a pre-visit report (PDF), export a backup, or export CSV files, you decide where they go — for example, to your doctor or to a file location. The app warns you that backups and exports contain health data. After you share a file, the recipient's privacy practices apply to their copy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything Pin2Win stored. Deleting the app does the same. Data in Apple Health stays in Apple Health; you can manage it in the Health app.

Medical disclaimer

Pin2Win is for patients following a testosterone therapy protocol prescribed and monitored by their physician. It is a personal log, not a medical device. It does not give medical advice, and it never calculates, converts or recommends a dose. Lab flags restate published guideline levels for discussion with your prescriber.
